# Next SOARA Saturday: Digital Modes at Gilleran Park

Ed Barnes, WA6ED, is giving members an early heads-up so they can get their
equipment set up and bring it to the park.

**What:** SOARA Saturday — theme is digital modes. Several members are
interested in a workshop for FreeDV and JS8Call on HF; this is a chance to
test various modes and features. Other digital modes are welcome too, as
well as UHF/VHF modes.

**When:** Saturday, September 19, 2026, 9:00 AM-noon

**Where:** Gilleran Park

Donuts will be provided. New to these modes? [JS8Call](http://js8call.com)
and [FreeDV](http://freedv.org) are both good starting points.

73,

Ed, WA6ED
