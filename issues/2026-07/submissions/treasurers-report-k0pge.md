## Summary

<!-- Editor-drafted from the figures for Ron's review — confirm or replace before
publishing. -->

Through the first nine months of the fiscal year, total cash income was
$10,238.14, down $3,092.86 from the same period a year earlier, driven mainly by
lower memberships and interest/other income. Total cash expenses were $11,163.78,
up $2,625.78 year over year, with increases across repeaters/site costs, member
activities, and other expenses. Cash net income for the period was
$(925.64), compared with $4,793.00 a year earlier. Ending cash on June 30, 2026
stood at $27,253.39.
