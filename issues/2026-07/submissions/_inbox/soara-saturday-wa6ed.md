SOARA Elmer Saturday June 2026
We had 11 attendees. Ed wa6ed, Greg n6pm, Tom w6trg, Ray k6nov, Greg we4by, Ray ae6h, Claude w6cag, Dale w6edt, Jim ki6lom, Patti ad6oh, Mike ko6aol.
We had donuts! $0 balance from last month. Donuts cost was $20. We took in $30. We have $10 for next month!
 
Donuts looked really good this month. I had to take a photo. Only a few holes and one donut left over. Thank you Greg n6pm for bringing them. Volunteers are always welcome at SOARA Saturday. 
Greg we4by demoed a kit he built that controls his HF rig. Later, Greg & Dale tested HF filters that Patti brought. These filters are from SK Howard Brown.
We had the big room. It's nice to spread out a bit. We also talked about AI which was interesting.
Ed wa6ed demoed a red Bird wattmeter avg and peak power. The Rak Wireless 1 watt showed about 0.7 watt on peak. Later at home, I observed 1 watt / 30dBm on the spectrum analyzer with a 60 dB attenuator.
For Field day I'm working on 4 demos for the education table. Ai helped me to add breadth to the summary. Worked out well.

1. Bioenno station to demo 12v power, This is how we power our radios.
2. SWR station to demo antenna with nano VNA, helps with understanding antenna and tuner.
3. RF Power station to demo RF power with a Bird wattmeter. RF power out.
4. Harmonic station to demo TinySA in harmonics mode. Helps with understanding FCC compliance.

Ed, WA6ED
