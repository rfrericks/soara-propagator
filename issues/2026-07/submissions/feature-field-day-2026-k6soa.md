# K6SOA / SOARA — ARRL Field Day 2026 Report

**South Orange Amateur Radio Association**
*June 27–28, 2026*

## We Made It On the Air!

Another Field Day is in the books for the South Orange Amateur Radio Association,
and K6SOA delivered a solid 24-hour performance across 10 bands and three operating
modes. From the first contact at 11:00 AM PDT Saturday to the final QSO just after
10:00 AM Sunday, operators kept the chairs warm, the rigs hot, and the log growing.

When the dust settled, K6SOA had logged **660 QSOs** with **597 unique stations**
spanning **75 ARRL sections** plus DX — reaching all corners of the country and
landing contacts on four continents.

20 meters was the workhorse of the event, accounting for nearly half of all
contacts (325 QSOs, 49%). The 40 meter band was a close second with 158 QSOs,
particularly productive after dark. 15 meters surprised with a strong showing of
103 contacts, a welcome sign of improving solar conditions. The VHF/UHF contingent
made their mark too, with contacts on 2m, 1.25m, and 70cm adding some local flavor
to the log.

## Editor Notes

Received as a clean markdown file. Figures verified: the band/mode totals, the
phone/CW/digital column splits (487 / 104 / 69), the operator breakdown, and the
hourly rate table all sum to 660 exactly, and QSO points (487 + 208 + 138 = 833)
are internally consistent. Two items need attention before publication:

- **Date corrected.** The source header read "June 28–29, 2026," but ARRL Field Day
  2026 ran the weekend of **June 27–28** (Saturday/Sunday), which also matches the
  President's Message in this same issue (Friday June 26 setup, operations Sat–Sun).
  Changed the header to June 27–28, 2026. Please confirm.
- **Scoring method — verify.** Standard ARRL Field Day scoring is (QSO points ×
  power multiplier) + bonus points; sections are **not** a score multiplier in Field
  Day (unlike Sweepstakes). The "Sections worked (75 × 100) = 7,500" line and the
  resulting "8,333 Estimated Base Score" don't follow the ARRL Field Day formula.
  The **833 QSO points** and **75 sections worked** are solid, verified numbers;
  confirm how you want to present the overall score, or drop the 7,500 / 8,333 lines,
  before publishing.
- **Byline.** No author is named (signed "73 de K6SOA"; "compiled from the contact
  log"). Confirm who compiled it and how to credit them.
- **Callsigns.** 17 operator calls plus 5 DX calls to spot-check. Verified against
  the roster where possible: WE4BY, K6UVR, K0PGE, AE6H, W6EDT, KO6AOL are club
  members; the rest aren't in the officer list — confirm.
- **"Four continents."** The five DX contacts span Europe, South America, Asia, and
  Oceania (four DX continents); with North America that's five continents including
  home. The intro's "four continents" is fine but you may want to say "five
  continents including North America" for punch.
- **Minor label clarity.** In the Score Summary, the CW/Digital rows show *points*
  (208, 138), not QSO counts (104, 69); I added the multiplier to each row label to
  make that explicit.
