# SOARA Propagator — Working Rules for Claude

These rules apply to all work in this repository, on every issue.

## Contributor content is preserved as written (do not abridge)

When a member submits an article, report, or announcement, publish it **as it was
written and intended.** You MAY fix spelling, grammar, punctuation, and formatting.
You MAY NOT change the content: do not abridge, condense, summarize, shorten,
reorder, rewrite, or cut an author's material, and do not drop their tables,
figures, sections, or examples. Include the **full** submission.

- If something is factually wrong or raises a concern (a date, a callsign, a score,
  a technical or legal claim, reprint permission, tone toward a named party), **do
  not silently change it.** Keep the author's text intact and raise it separately in
  the submission's "Editor Notes" and to the editor, so the author or editor
  decides.
- Raw material clearly submitted for write-up (e.g., informal meeting notes) may be
  organized into readable prose, but still without dropping any of the substance.
- Preserve the author's voice, byline, and sign-off.

Length is not a reason to cut. If an article is long, run it in full; do not
shorten it to save space without the editor's explicit instruction.

## Treasurer's report comes from the marked block in the ledger

Every month the Treasurer submits the club's **full general ledger** as an Excel
workbook (for example `SOARA General Ledger FY 2025-2026 as of July 31, 2026.xlsx`).
Most of that workbook is internal club bookkeeping and is **not** for publication.

Use **only** the block the Treasurer has marked off and labeled **"Financial
Statement for Propagator."** It sits to the right of the monthly ledger columns on the
first sheet and already contains exactly the figures intended for members: the current
and prior year columns, the increase/(decrease) column, beginning and ending cash, and
the Treasurer's standard closing note. Pull that section and nothing else — do not mine
the ledger for extra detail, do not add accounts, and do not restructure the
presentation.

Then:

- **Reproduce the figures exactly**, in the table format used by the most recently
  published issue, so consecutive issues read consistently.
- **Recompute the subtotals to confirm they tie out**, and check continuity against the
  previous issue's ending cash. Record what was checked in Editor Notes.
- If a figure does not tie out, **flag it — do not fix it.** The Treasurer's marked
  block is what gets printed; discrepancies go to the Treasurer to resolve. Same rule
  as for any other contributor's content.
- Keep `approved_for_publication: false` until the Treasurer has approved the section
  as it will appear. A ledger workbook is raw data, not an approved report. Any
  narrative paragraph the editor drafts from those figures needs his sign-off before it
  runs.

`templates/financial-report.md` carries the same instruction inline.

See `docs/editorial-style.md` and `docs/generation-guide.md` for the full workflow.
