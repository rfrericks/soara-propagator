## Recurring Editorial Pattern

- Open with club leadership and the next membership meeting.
- Follow with recent club activity and photos.
- Use the middle of the issue for timely announcements, links, and member
  articles.
- Close with the treasurer's report, a two-month event calendar, and evergreen
  club information.

The published PDFs remain the visual references for branding, page layout,
headers, footers, and image treatment.
